Custom Web Development

Custom web development is a site that does real work — not a brochure with a contact form.

Resonant designs and builds custom websites around how your business sells, serves, and operates. That means architecture first: the pages, flows, and systems that match the work — not a theme with your logo swapped in.

We ship sites that are fast, searchable, and ready to connect to the tools you already use. When you need automation after the click, that lives beside this work in System Integrations. When you need search that pulls weight, that is SEO Foundations.

What gets built

  • Custom pages and templates matched to your offer and sales process.
  • Application-style functionality when the site has to do more than inform.
  • CRM and API connections so leads and data move without retyping.
  • Dashboards and reporting when operators need visibility, not vanity charts.
  • Content architecture built for search — one clear page per intent cluster.

How this fits the site build

Custom development sits at the center. SEO Foundations make the page map and technical baseline search-ready. System Integrations wire the site to CRM, AI phone, and follow-up. Impulse by Design is the automation partner when the work after the website is the bottleneck.
